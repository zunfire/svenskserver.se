Survive if you can.

EXTRAHARD MEDIEVAL is a small Swedish Minecraft server for friends, where survival is for real. Monsters are stronger, caves are deadlier and the night is a time for battle.

The world is kept in order by Smeden. You rarely see Smeden, but Smeden sees you.

Extra hard

ExtraHardMode makes the world tougher: more dangerous monsters, caves that collapse and less forgiving survival.

Protect your build

Claim your land with a golden shovel (GriefPrevention). Nobody else can build or take anything there without your permission.

PvP at night

Daytime is peaceful. When the sun goes down players can hurt each other, so stay home or keep your sword ready.

What makes it extra hard?

The server runs ExtraHardMode – something few servers dare to do. Here Minecraft is genuinely hard.

  • The rock is hard. Stone and deepslate only give way to copper pickaxes or better, and they wear out fast. No digging 1×1 straight down here: find natural caves, follow the ore veins where the rock softens, or blast your way through. Blasted stone turns to cobblestone.
  • Darkness in the deep. Torches fail below Y 30, so bring lanterns. Above ground the rain puts them out.
  • Monsters fight dirty. Zombies rise again and slow you down. Skeletons shoot fireballs and silverfish. Creepers can be charged and sometimes drop TNT.
  • Your body takes less. Falls hurt twice as much, and heavy armour slows you down and drags you under water.
  • The land is stingy. Crops can die, deserts are barren and snow kills the fields.
  • The night is worst. When the sun goes down it gets harder than usual, and PvP is allowed.
  • Death costs. A tenth of what you carry is lost forever, and you wake up in the nearest jail.
