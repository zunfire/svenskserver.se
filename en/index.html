Survive if you can.

EXTRAHARD MEDIEVAL is a small Swedish Minecraft server for friends, where survival is for real. Monsters are stronger, caves are deadlier and the night is a time for battle.

The world is kept in order by Smeden. You rarely see Smeden, but Smeden sees you.

Extra hard

ExtraHardMode makes the world tougher: more dangerous monsters, caves that collapse and less forgiving survival.

Protect your build

Claim your land with a golden shovel (GriefPrevention). Nobody else can build or take anything there without your permission.

PvP at night

Daytime is peaceful. When the sun goes down players can hurt each other, so stay home or keep your sword ready.
